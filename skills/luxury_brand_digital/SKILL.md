# Luxury Brand Digital Playbook

## Overview
This playbook captures proven digital strategy principles for luxury and premium brand launches, distilled from TSF engagements with Somi London (luxury resortwear, UK-to-India launch) and Jatin Kothari (Indian couture maison). These principles are applicable to any high-AOV, exclusivity-driven brand.

---

## 1. The "Un-Ad" Principle

> Ultra-premium buyers don't want to be "sold to." They want to *discover* the brand.

### Rules
- Traditional polished ads suffer from ad fatigue and banner blindness at this price point
- Use **organic-looking, story-driven content** amplified through paid distribution
- Content should feel like editorial, not advertising
- Behind-the-scenes, craftsmanship process, and founder stories outperform catalog/product shots
- UGC-style Reels consistently outperform studio-polished content for luxury engagement

### What to Avoid
- ❌ Hard CTAs ("Shop Now," "Buy," "Order Today")
- ❌ Discount/sale/offer-based messaging (destroys luxury positioning)
- ❌ Product-grid style content (feels like mass retail)
- ❌ Trending audio reels, meme content, hashtag challenges
- ❌ Price mentions in any public-facing content

### What to Use Instead
- ✅ Elegant prompts: "Explore the Collection," "Visit the Atelier," "Book a Private Appointment," "Request an Invitation"
- ✅ First-person voice expressing passion for craft: "We honor India's legacy of artisans…"
- ✅ Words: "discover," "experience," "atelier," "legacy," "story," "journey," "craft"
- ✅ Show-don't-tell: visuals of fabric sheen, artisan hands, heritage settings > copy describing "high quality"

## 2. Surgical Ad Spend Strategy

### Budget Framework
For brands with AOV of ₹50,000+, a surgical monthly ad spend of **₹50K-1L** is sufficient and efficient.

| AOV Range | Recommended Monthly Ad Spend | Rationale |
|:---|:---|:---|
| ₹25K-50K | ₹50K-1L/month | Single conversion covers 50-200% of monthly spend |
| ₹50K-1L | ₹50K-75K/month | Single conversion covers 100%+ of monthly spend |
| ₹1L+ | ₹25K-50K/month | Even minimal conversions are profitable |

### Channel Allocation
| Channel | % of Budget | Purpose |
|:---|:---|:---|
| Meta (Instagram) | 70% | Brand awareness + retargeting + appointment bookings |
| Google Search | 30% | Brand name capture + high-intent keyword targeting |

### What NOT to Spend On
- ❌ Google Display Network (low-quality impressions for luxury)
- ❌ YouTube pre-roll (insufficient budget for meaningful luxury reach)
- ❌ Broad prospecting to cold audiences (waste at this AOV)
- ❌ Influencer marketplace ads (dilutes brand exclusivity)

### Retargeting Strategy
- Build retargeting pools from website visitors, IG engagers, and video viewers
- Retargeting budget should be 30-40% of total Meta spend
- Lookalike audiences from appointment bookers are the highest-value seed

## 3. Content Pillar Framework

Applicable to any luxury artisan/craft brand's social media:

| Pillar | % of Content | Description | Examples |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **The Craft** | 35% | Process, artisanship, behind-the-scenes, materials | Close-up of embroidery, weaver at loom, fabric detail, hands at work |
| **The Collection** | 30% | Product photography, editorial-style imagery | Styled garments in heritage settings, detail shots |
| **The Story** | 20% | Brand heritage, cultural references, founder's vision | Architecture, heritage motifs, literary references |
| **The World** | 15% | Lifestyle context, mood, atmosphere | Palace interiors, nature textures, curated still-lifes |

### Feed Aesthetic Rules
- **Grid Layout:** Alternating detail shots and wide compositions
- **Color Consistency:** Warm neutrals (ivory, stone, sand) with deep accent tones (maroon, emerald, lapis)
- **Typography on Posts:** Minimal to none. Let imagery speak.
- **Captions:** Poetic, short. Emotive language, never salesy.

## 4. Appointment-Based Conversion Funnel

For brands that sell by appointment (couture, bespoke, private viewings):

### Primary KPI: Appointment Inquiries (NOT transactions)

| Funnel Stage | Metric | Tool |
|:---|:---|:---|
| Awareness | Profile visits, reach, impressions | Meta Ads Manager |
| Interest | Website visits, scroll depth, time on site | GA4 |
| Consideration | Collection page views, lookbook engagement | GA4 custom events |
| Intent | Appointment CTA clicks | GTM event tracking |
| Conversion | Appointment booked | Calendly/booking system |
| Post-Conversion | Client relationship, repeat | CRM / WhatsApp |

### WhatsApp Concierge (Future Upsell)
- For brands with high cart abandonment or complex purchase journeys
- Dedicated styling concierge via WhatsApp Business API
- High-touch sales for high-ticket items
- Can recover 15%+ of abandoned inquiries (from SOMI data)

## 5. Quality-Over-Volume Metrics

For luxury brands, standard marketing metrics are misleading. Optimize for engagement quality:

### Metrics That Matter
| Metric | Why | Target |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **Average Session Duration** | Measures "lingering" — the brand wants visitors to stay | >2 minutes |
| **Scroll Depth (75%+)** | Indicates content is compelling enough to read fully | >40% of visitors |
| **Appointment Inquiry Rate** | Primary conversion event | >1% of visitors |
| **Return Visitor Rate** | Brand is memorable enough to revisit | >20% |
| **Instagram Save Rate** | Strongest engagement signal (content worth revisiting) | >2% per post |

### Metrics That Don't Matter (for luxury)
- ❌ Pageviews (volume ≠ quality)
- ❌ Bounce rate (luxury visitors may browse one page deeply)
- ❌ Follower count (100 engaged HNIs > 10,000 mass followers)
- ❌ Like count (vanity metric)

## 6. Content Bank Strategy

### Pre-Launch Rule
> **Never launch social media without a 30-day content bank.**

### Process
1. **Weeks 2-3 before launch:** Batch-edit all raw content from client (photos, videos)
2. **Deliverable:** 30+ pieces of ready-to-post content (posts, reels, stories)
3. **Buffer:** Always maintain 2-week content buffer after launch
4. **Content Calendar:** Prepared monthly, approved bi-weekly by client

### Content Editing Scope (When TSF Handles Editing, Not Production)
- Color grading and retouching of raw photos
- Video trimming, subtitling, and compression
- Web optimization (resolution, file size, format)
- Social media format adaptation (square, portrait, story dimensions)
- NOT: photoshoots, video production, model booking, location scouting

## 8. Ownership & Infrastructure Principles

### Golden Rule
> **The client ALWAYS owns all digital infrastructure. The agency gets admin access to manage.**

### Ownership Checklist
| Asset | Owner | Agency Access Level |
|:---|:---|:---|
| Domain name | Client | DNS admin |
| Web hosting | Client | Deployment access |
| Google Analytics | Client (client email) | Editor role |
| Google Tag Manager | Client (client email) | Admin container access |
| Meta Business Suite | Client | Admin role |
| Instagram account | Client | Content manager |
| Google Ads account | Client (client email) | Manager role |
| Ad spend billing | Client pays direct | — |

### Why This Matters
- Prevents vendor lock-in
- Client retains full data history if agency relationship ends
- Builds trust with sophisticated clients who understand digital ownership
- Avoids legal complications around data and account ownership

## 9. Competitive Positioning for Luxury Brands

### Positioning Quadrant Template
When analyzing competitors, plot them on two axes:
- **X-axis:** Mass Market ↔ Ultra-Exclusive
- **Y-axis:** Trend-Driven ↔ Heritage-Driven

The target position for artisanal luxury brands is always **top-right** (ultra-exclusive + heritage-driven).

### Competitive Advantages to Highlight
- Origin story (London-made, heritage Indian, etc.)
- Scarcity (limited edition, made-to-order, appointment-only)
- Craftsmanship credentials (specific techniques, artisan communities)
- Cultural authenticity (not borrowed aesthetic)
- Price positioning relative to competitors
