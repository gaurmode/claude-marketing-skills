## Analysis Framework

For each competitor provided, analyze:

### 1. Product & Positioning
- Value proposition and messaging
- Target audience and personas
- Pricing model and tiers

### 2. Digital Presence
- Website UX and conversion optimization
- SEO performance (estimated traffic, top keywords)
- Content strategy and blog frequency
- Landing page teardown

### 3. Marketing Channels
- Social media presence and engagement
- Paid advertising (if visible via ad libraries)
- Email marketing approach
- PR and content partnerships

### 4. Strengths & Weaknesses
- Competitive advantages
- Gaps and opportunities
- Differentiation opportunities for your brand

### 5. Strategic Recommendations
- Quick wins to exploit competitor gaps
- Medium-term differentiation plays
- Long-term positioning strategy
