## Campaign Plan Template

### 1. Campaign Brief
- Objective (awareness / leads / conversions / retention)
- Target audience segments
- Budget allocation
- Timeline and milestones
- Key success metrics (KPIs)

### 2. Channel Strategy
For each relevant channel, define:
- **Paid**: Google Ads, Meta Ads, LinkedIn Ads — targeting, budgets, creatives
- **Organic**: SEO content, social posts, community engagement
- **Email**: Sequences, segments, automation triggers
- **PR/Partnerships**: Outreach targets and messaging

### 3. Creative Assets
- Ad copy variations (3-5 per platform)
- Landing page copy and wireframe
- Email sequence drafts
- Social media post calendar

### 4. Measurement Plan
- Tracking setup (UTMs, pixels, events)
- Reporting dashboard design
- A/B test plan
- Optimization triggers and decision criteria

### 5. Timeline
- Week-by-week execution plan
- Launch checklist
- Post-launch optimization schedule
